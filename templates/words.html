Wise word

From Dallas, They Might Be Giants makes the quick drive to Albany; futuristic capital of the state of New York. Lost in the city's downtown, a concrete shrine to Nelson Rockefeller's vision of dehuamizing bureaucracy. The band soon find themselves confronted by the egg.

The egg.

More than just a venue, the egg is a giant cement performace orb whose smooth, featureless exterior suggests a worrisome lack of fire exits.
